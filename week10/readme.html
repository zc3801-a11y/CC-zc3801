Week 10 Gestural control

I used p5.js to draw a top-down view of a cocktail glass, layering circles in warm red and amber tones to create the color of the drink. I used ml5.js HandPose with my webcam to track the position of my index fingertip. The camera feed is hidden, and a small glowing dot shows where my finger is on the canvas. I compare the fingertip’s current position with its previous position to determine which way I’m stirring, then rotate the ice cubes and lemon slice in that direction. When I stop moving my finger, I use lerp() to gradually reduce the rotation speed so the ice cubes and lemon slice keep drifting for a moment, like they would in a real glass.
