Week 6 Abstract Clock Final

I took visual inspiration from PolarClock and bar charts to create a horizontal abstract clock with three bars of different thicknesses. In the second week, I divided the seconds bar into small blocks that appear one by one and reset at the start of each minute. I also added a different-colored block for each completed hour, and those blocks reset at midnight. I kept the minute bar as one continuous shape that grows throughout the hour and resets when a new hour begins. I added `lerp()` to make the transitions smoother as part of the second week’s assignment.
